⚠ Warnings this month
Supervisors give warnings from the worker card. Every 4th warning in a month deducts 4 h from basic hours automatically. Removing a warning also takes back a 4 h deduction that is no longer due. The list starts fresh each month.

⏱ Penalty – deduct hours or days
Hours: taken off the worker's normal hours for that day, never off OT. Days: that many days of basic pay (basic ÷ 30 × days) are deducted on that month's payslip. The reason is saved as a note in the Daily Log.
✈ Annual vacations
30 days every year (UAE Labour Law Art. 29). Kept in the ANNUAL VACATION tab of the salary sheet – updated by itself. Next eligible = 1 year after the last day of the last vacation (or after joining). Tap a name for the full history or to add a vacation.
